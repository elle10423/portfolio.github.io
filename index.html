IT Student

When I started this course, I had no real curiosity about coding or prototyping. But as time passed, it caught my attention, especially creating UI/UX designs, and now I find myself drawn to making layouts that look good and feel easy to use.

Outside of class I enjoy drawing, painting, and reading books. Sketching and painting let me experiment with color and composition, and I often bring those ideas into my UI sketches. I keep a small notebook of UI ideas I want to try, and I work best when a task has a clear goal and I can test it as I go.

Where I am now

I'm an early-stage learner. I can lay out a responsive page, style it consistently, wire it into a small Django project, and publish it — but I still check documentation for anything unfamiliar, and I don't yet build production backend systems.
